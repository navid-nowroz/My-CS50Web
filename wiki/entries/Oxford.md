# Oxford

**University of Oxford** is a *collegiate research university* located in **Oxford, England**.  
Tracing its origins back to *1096*, Oxford is recognized as the **oldest university in the English-speaking world**, renowned for its traditions, academic excellence, and influential alumni across politics, science, and literature.
